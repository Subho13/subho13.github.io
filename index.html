About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at IIT Kharagpur, where I build generative AI models that accelerate the discovery of new crystalline materials.

My work spans equivariant graph neural networks, latent diffusion models, autoregressive language models, and flow matching, applied to crystal structure generation and property-conditioned inverse design, that is, generating stable materials that have a desired target property.
